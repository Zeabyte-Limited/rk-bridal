# Content guide for the studio team

This site is built to win Google searches like "bridal makeup artist in Jalandhar" and turn them into WhatsApp
enquiries. Three things make that work: **real photos, real reviews, and honest claims.** Here is how to feed it.

## 1. Photos & videos (replace the stock placeholders ASAP)

- Every launch photo/video is licensed stock, chosen to show the design. Google and brides can tell the difference —
  real work converts far better.
- Send 30–60 of your best bride/groom/couple/hair/mehndi photos (full-res, from the photographer, with the couple's OK).
- Short vertical reels (5–15 s, no music needed) of: a bride turning to camera, airbrush in action, a dupatta being
  set, a groom's final look, a sangeet. These become the hero videos.
- Captions describe the LOOK ("Anand Karaj morning, HD base, classic red"), never a client's name unless they have agreed.

## 2. Reviews — the 50+ plan

The site has a reviews system designed for 50+ cards with photos. **Only real reviews go in.** Fake or invented reviews
are illegal in India (Consumer Protection Act 2019 + the 2023 fake-review guidelines), get Google listings suspended,
and are the fastest way to lose a bride's trust. Real ones are easy to collect:

1. After every event, WhatsApp the bride (and the groom, and the mum) the next day: "Would you leave us two lines and
   one photo we can share?" Most say yes within an hour.
2. Ask past hair clients too — they count, and they already love you.
3. Also ask for a **Google review** (set up the Google Business Profile first) — those rank the business on Maps.
4. Send each review + photo + permission ("yes, you can use my name and photo") to Parminder; it goes into
   `src/data/testimonials.ts` and is live within minutes.

Target: 10 real reviews in the first month, 50 by the end of the first season.

## 3. What the site claims (keep it true)

- Trial included with bridal bookings · hair + draping included · written quotes · no travel charge in Jalandhar.
  If any of these change, tell Parminder the same day so the site changes too.
- No prices are published yet. When a rate card is agreed, "from ₹…" can go on the Packages page.
- The About page describes the team by role (lead artist, second artist, hair, draping, mehndi, assistant). Add names and
  photos when the team is confirmed.

## 4. Google Business Profile (do this week)

Create/claim the profile for the studio address in Jalandhar, category "Makeup artist" + "Bridal shop", add the website
link, 20+ photos, services, and the WhatsApp number. Post once a week. This is the single biggest local-SEO lever.

## 5. Instagram

Post daily in season. Every post: location tag (Jalandhar / venue), 3–5 hashtags, and "Book your date — link in bio".
The website's Portfolio and Contact pages link to the handle set in `src/config.ts`.
