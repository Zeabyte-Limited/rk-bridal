A Punjabi wedding is five to seven looks in a week, not one. Here is how we plan each one — the mood, the hair, the timing, and the mistakes to avoid.

## Roka & sagan — "like you, on a very good day"

The first family function, usually daytime, often at home. Natural skin, soft defined eyes, a lip that matches the suit. Hair: soft open waves or a neat low bun. Allow 90 minutes. Mistake to avoid: going full-glam this early; it sets an exhausting bar for the week.

## Engagement — soft glam

Ring ceremony, evening hall lighting, lots of close-up photos of the hands. Dewy skin, a shimmer lid, defined lashes, a sleek bun or a half-up style that frames the face. Allow two hours. Make sure the hands are prepped — moisturised, nails done — the ring photos are all hands.

## Mehndi — dewy and festive

Afternoon, yellow and green outfits, florals, lots of movement. Light, dewy base; colour on the lips; open hair with fresh flowers or a floral braid. Keep the base light because you will be sitting with wet mehndi for hours and you will sweat. Allow 90 minutes. Mehndi note: do your hands first, then makeup — never the other way round.

## Haldi — barely there

Haldi gets on everything. Minimal base (or none), waterproof mascara, a tinted lip, hair tied back or in a loose braid. A proper skin prep the night before means the turmeric comes off without staining.

## Sangeet — glam that dances

The big night out. Long-wear base, strong eyes, lashes, a hairstyle that holds through giddha and bhangra — a secured braid or a pinned bun beats open hair every time. Allow two hours. Sweat-proof everything; set the base properly.

## Anand Karaj / pheras — the look that lasts till the doli

The earliest start and the longest wear. Classic tones — red, maroon, rust — a timeless eye (not a trend), modest-yet-glowing skin, and hair that can carry a heavy dupatta, kaleere and a maang tikka. Draping is pinned for matha tek and hours of sitting. Allow three hours and expect a 4–5 am start for a morning ceremony. Chooda goes on after the base is set. A standby artist for the doli refresh is worth it.

## Reception — a new look, not a touch-up

Evening, flash photography, a completely different outfit. We take the morning look off and start again: sculpted skin, a bolder eye, a fresh lip, a new hairstyle. Allow two hours. Flash-test everything.

## Putting it together

Book all the events in one go: the same artists learn your face and the looks stay consistent. At the trial we design the wedding-day look in full and map the others around it. And give yourself one evening in the middle of the week with no makeup at all — your skin will thank you on the morning that matters.
