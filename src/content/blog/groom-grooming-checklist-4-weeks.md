Brides plan for months. Grooms often plan for an hour — the morning of. Four weeks of small, easy steps makes a very visible difference in the photos you will be looking at for the rest of your life.

## Four weeks out

- **Start a basic routine:** cleanser morning and night, a light moisturiser, sunscreen every day. That is it. Do not buy a twelve-step kit.
- **Decide the beard shape** with your barber now, so there is time to grow or trim into it.
- **Book your grooming** for every event you care about: pre-wedding shoot, wedding, reception.
- **Hands and nails:** the ring photos are coming. One tidy manicure now and one the week before.

## Two weeks out

- **Haircut plan:** book the final cut for 7–10 days before the wedding.
- **Sleep and water:** boring, but dark circles are the number one thing we correct on grooms.
- **Try on the sherwani / suit** with the shoes and the safa or dastaar. Note anything that pulls or shows (a collar that rubs the neck, a sehra that flattens hair).
- **No new products** from here. A reaction a week before the wedding is a disaster you can avoid.

## The final week

- **Final haircut** (and beard shape).
- **Lips:** balm every night. Dry lips look dramatically worse on camera than in person.
- **Brows:** tidy strays only — do not reshape.
- **Sun:** avoid getting burnt; tan lines from a sleeveless jersey show in an open-collar kurta.

## The day

We arrive about 90 minutes before you need to be ready. Here is what groom grooming actually involves:

1. **Skin:** cleanse, moisturise, a sheer matte base matched to your neck. Dark circles and razor marks evened out. Shine controlled for the lights and the dhol-side sweat.
2. **Beard and brows:** lined, conditioned, tidy.
3. **Hair:** styled to last. Dastaar-ready or sehra-safe — we style around the headgear, not under it.
4. **The sherwani check:** collar, kalgi, brooch and dupatta straight. The back too — the photographer will shoot it.
5. **A touch-up kit** for your brother or best friend: blotting sheets, a comb, lip balm.

## What it is not

Not foundation you can see. Not a tan. Not eyeliner (unless you want it). Grooms who book us say the same thing afterwards: nobody noticed anything, and the photos were the best they have ever taken. That is the whole point.
