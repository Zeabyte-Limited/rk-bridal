The trial is the most important two hours of your bridal makeup — more important than the wedding morning itself, because the morning is just a repeat of what we decide here. This is how ours works and how to get the most from it.

## When

Two to six weeks before the wedding. Late enough that the outfit, jewellery and hairstyle ideas are final; early enough to fix anything (a skin issue, a hair extension colour) before the day. Avoid booking the trial straight after a facial or a new skincare product.

## What to bring

- **The outfit** — or clear photos of it, including the dupatta.
- **The jewellery** — the actual set if you can; the maang tikka and earrings change how we do hair and eyes.
- **Reference looks** — three you love and, just as useful, one you hate.
- **The venue** — a photo or the name, so we know the lighting (daylight gurdwara vs. tented night function).
- **The timeline** — ceremony time, when the photographer wants you ready, when the doli is.
- **Your skin history** — allergies, sensitivity, anything that has reacted before.

## What we do

1. **Skin check** under studio light and with a camera flash. This decides [HD vs airbrush](/blog/hd-vs-airbrush-makeup-punjabi-bride/) and the prep routine for the next few weeks.
2. **Colour match** to your neck and chest, not your face.
3. **The full look** — base, eyes, lashes, lips, hair and the dupatta set exactly as it will be on the day.
4. **Camera test** — phone photos with and without flash, indoors and at the window. If something flashes back or looks grey, we fix it now.
5. **Notes** — every product, shade and step is written down. On the wedding morning the artist is working from a recipe, not a memory.

## Questions to ask at the trial

- How long will this look last, and what will I need for touch-ups?
- What skincare should I do (and stop) between now and the wedding?
- What time will you arrive on the day, and how many people are coming?
- If I want to change something after today, how do we do that?

## What to do afterwards

Wear the look for the rest of the day. Go out in the sun, eat, drink, take photos at 6 pm. That is the real test. Then message us with what you loved and what bothered you. "The lip felt dry by evening" is exactly the feedback that makes the wedding morning perfect.

## The one mistake to avoid

Do not trial a look you would never wear. Brides sometimes ask for "something different" at the trial and then panic on the morning. Trial the look you actually want. The day is for being yourself — only more so.
