Your makeup artist is the last person who touches your face before you walk out. Choosing well is less about the most followers and more about the right answers to a few direct questions. Here are the twelve we would want every bride in Jalandhar to ask — including of us.

## 1. "Is my date actually free?"

Start here. Ask for a written confirmation of the date and the start time once you pay the advance. Peak-season artists double up; make sure you are the only bride on that morning, or that the second bride has a different team.

## 2. "Will you do a trial, and is it included?"

A trial is where the look is designed, tested under a camera flash and written down. If the answer is "we don't need one, we've done hundreds of brides," keep looking. Ask who does the trial — it should be the same artist who will be with you on the day.

## 3. "HD or airbrush — and why for me?"

A good artist will ask about your skin, the season and the length of your day before answering. If the answer is one technique for everyone, that is a sales pitch, not a recommendation. Read our [HD vs airbrush guide](/blog/hd-vs-airbrush-makeup-punjabi-bride/) before the conversation.

## 4. "What exactly is included in the price?"

Get the list: base, eyes, lashes, hair, draping, jewellery setting, touch-up kit, an artist on standby. Hair and draping are the two things most often quoted separately. Ask for the quote in writing on WhatsApp.

## 5. "Who is actually coming on the day?"

Team size matters more than people think. One artist cannot do a bride, her mum and two sisters by 6 am. Ask how many people arrive, what each does, and whether the lead artist personally does the bride.

## 6. "What time will you arrive, and how long will I take?"

Hair, makeup and draping for a bride take around three hours. For a morning Anand Karaj the team should be with you before dawn. Ask how they build the timeline and whether they talk to your photographer.

## 7. "What products do you use?"

You are looking for professional, long-wear products and a sensible answer about your skin type. If you have sensitive skin or allergies, say so now — and ask them to patch-test at the trial.

## 8. "Can I see full-day photos, not just the first one?"

Everyone looks good at 8 am. Ask to see a bride at the doli or the reception. Real, unretouched phone photos from a family member are worth more than the photographer's edited frames.

## 9. "Do you travel, and what does it cost?"

If the wedding is in Phagwara, Ludhiana or Chandigarh and the artist is in Jalandhar, ask for the travel charge as a separate line and whether an overnight stay is needed for early ceremonies.

## 10. "What happens if you fall ill?"

Nobody likes this question; good studios have an answer. A team-based studio has a trained second artist who already knows your written look. A solo artist should be able to name their backup.

## 11. "What is the advance and the cancellation policy?"

Expect a fixed advance to hold the date, the balance before the wedding, and a non-refundable advance close to the date. That is normal. What is not normal is no written terms at all.

## 12. "Can I speak to a recent bride?"

Reviews are easy to write; references are not. An artist who is proud of their work will happily connect you with a bride from last month.

## The one thing not to do

Do not book on the basis of a single stunning Instagram reel. Reels are edited, filtered and lit. Book on the basis of a trial, a written quote and a clear plan for your morning. That is what a wedding day actually needs.
