## Recommendation

In Indian bridal makeup the strongest brands are **personal names** ("Makeup by Simran Kaur", "Parul Garg Makeup").
Brides book a person, and Google ranks "<name> makeup artist jalandhar" easily. So the best option is
**"<Her first name> Makeup Artistry"** or **"Makeup by <Her name>"**, with "RK" as the studio/team brand underneath if wanted.

If a non-personal brand is preferred, pick something short, Punjabi-flavoured and easy to say on the phone.

## Domain advice

- Buy **.com AND .in** of the chosen name (~NZ$30/yr total) and point both at the Cloudflare Worker; .com primary.
- Register through Cloudflare Registrar (at-cost, no upsells) so DNS + Worker custom domain live in one place.
- Grab the matching **Instagram handle** before buying the domain — Instagram matters more than the domain for a bridal MUA.
