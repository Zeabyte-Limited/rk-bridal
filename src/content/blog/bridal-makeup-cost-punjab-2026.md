Price is the question every bride wants to ask first and feels awkward asking. So here is the honest version: what the ranges look like, what actually drives the number, and what should always be in it.

## The ranges, broadly

Wedding-industry guides for India in 2026 describe four tiers for the wedding day (bride only):

- **Entry:** roughly ₹8,000–₹25,000
- **Mid-range HD:** roughly ₹25,000–₹60,000
- **Premium (airbrush, multi-event, full team):** roughly ₹60,000–₹1.5 lakh
- **Celebrity-tier:** ₹1.5 lakh and up

Big metros sit higher; Punjab's cities — Jalandhar, Ludhiana, Amritsar, Chandigarh — tend to sit in the lower-middle of the national ranges for comparable quality. These are market observations, not our rate card: our quotes are written for your specific events, and we send them on WhatsApp.

## What actually moves the number

1. **Number of events.** The wedding day is the anchor; engagement, sangeet, mehndi and reception are each priced lower, and a multi-event package is cheaper per event than booking one by one.
2. **Technique.** Airbrush usually costs more than HD because of equipment, products and time.
3. **Team size.** A lead artist plus hair, draping and an assistant costs more than a solo artist — and finishes a bride and her family on time.
4. **Hair and draping.** Included by some, extra with others. Ask.
5. **Trial.** Included, chargeable, or adjusted against the booking — all three exist.
6. **Travel.** Within the artist's city is often free; other cities are a line item; destination weddings include travel and stay.
7. **Season and day.** Peak dates (November–February, April–May weekends, muhurat days) book out and sometimes cost more.

## What should always be included

- A trial, with the same artist who does the day.
- Skin prep, base, eyes, lashes, lips, contour.
- A clear statement on hair and draping — in or out.
- A touch-up kit.
- Written terms: advance, balance, cancellation, arrival time.

## Red flags in a cheap quote

No trial. No written quote. One artist for a bride plus family on a 5 am start. "We'll decide the products on the day." A price that changes once you have paid the advance.

## Red flags in an expensive quote

Vague inclusions. A famous name who sends an assistant on the day. A trial charged at full price with nothing adjusted. Travel and "team charges" that appear after the fact.

## How to compare two quotes properly

Line them up item by item: events, technique, hair, draping, trial, team size, travel, standby artist. Two quotes that differ by ₹15,000 often differ by a hair stylist, a trial and a second artist — which is to say, by a calm morning versus a stressful one.
