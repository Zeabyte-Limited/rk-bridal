Punjab's wedding season peaks when the weather is at its driest. December fog, single-digit mornings and heaters running all night pull the moisture out of skin exactly when you need it to glow. Here is how to prepare, and how we adapt the look.

## Four weeks out: hydrate from both sides

- **Water:** boring, essential. Dehydrated skin shows texture under any base.
- **A richer moisturiser** at night. Switch from your summer gel to a cream.
- **Gentle exfoliation** twice a week to lift the dry flakes a base would otherwise sit on.
- **Lips:** balm every night from now. Chapped lips cannot be covered; they can only be prevented.
- **Hands:** mehndi takes better on hydrated skin, and the chooda photos are all hands.

## Two weeks out: no experiments

No new facials, peels, serums or "brightening" treatments. A reaction now is a reaction on your wedding morning. If you have a trusted facial routine, do it now and not later.

## The last three days

Rich moisturiser morning and night, a hydrating sheet mask the evening before the wedding, and no exfoliation in the final 48 hours. Sleep. Avoid salty food the night before (puffy eyes at 5 am are the enemy).

## How we adapt the look for winter

**The base.** Dry winter skin wants a hydrating, satin-finish base — not the matte, oil-controlling formula we reach for in May. At the trial we test [HD against airbrush](/blog/hd-vs-airbrush-makeup-punjabi-bride/) on your actual skin, and in winter HD often wins.

**Prep on the day.** A proper skin-prep layer before anything else: a hydrating primer, a moment to let it sink in, then base. Rushing this step is how makeup goes patchy by lunchtime.

**Setting.** Lighter setting powder, placed only where you crease and shine, and a hydrating setting spray. Winter is not the time to bake.

**Cheeks and glow.** Cream blush and cream highlighter melt into dry skin where powders can look dusty.

**Lips.** A hydrating liner, a satin lip, and a balm in the touch-up kit. Matte liquid lips are tempting for longevity and brutal on winter lips.

**Hair.** Static and frizz are real in dry air. We work with a little serum, and heavy dupattas are pinned into a secure base so the fog outside and the heaters inside do not undo the hairstyle.

## The fog factor

Winter mornings in Jalandhar, Ludhiana and Amritsar can be delayed by fog — baraat timings slip, ceremonies start late. We build a buffer into the morning and bring the touch-up kit for exactly that reason. Beautiful at 7 am, still beautiful whenever the laavan actually begin.

A December wedding is one of the most beautiful times to be a Punjabi bride — the light, the shawls, the warmth of a tented sangeet. With four weeks of simple prep, your skin will be ready for it.
